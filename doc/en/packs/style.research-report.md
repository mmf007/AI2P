# Research and reporting

**Pack code:** `style.research-report` · **Records:** 10 · **Skills:** `analyze-data`,
`analyze-requirements`, `analyze-plan`, `text-docs`, `text-summarize`

## What this style is

The discipline of work that has no test run: a market survey, a comparison of options, an
analysis of someone else's solution, a briefing for a decision. There is nothing to verify such
work with, so its quality is set by rules — a source for every fact, a number with its unit and
date, the conclusion at the top, the «what was not done» section at the bottom.

## Who it is for

Everyone who hands analysis and surveys to an AI and then decides by them. This is the most
common kind of task for an AI performer and the most dangerous one: the confident tone of a
report is exactly the same for a verified fact and for an invented one.

## What changes after the installation

* Every fact has a source and the date it was checked; a fact without a source is explicitly
  marked as an assumption.
* Identifiers, prices, limits and field names are verified against the primary source, not
  taken from a retelling or from the answer of a search model.
* The work starts with the single question the report must answer.
* The report opens with the conclusion: what to do or what to choose.
* **The «what was NOT done and not checked» section is mandatory in any report.**
* Options are compared in a table with the same columns; a rejected option stays together with
  the reason for the rejection.
* A number comes with its unit, the way it was obtained and the date of the measurement.
* The size of the report is set by the question, not by the amount of material collected.
* There is one recommendation, stated plainly, with its risks beside it.
* Only a link that was actually opened is given.

## What is worth adjusting

* **The record about the «what was not done» section is the only one in the distribution marked
  «always load».** It reaches EVERY task of the organisation regardless of the skill: the rule
  holds for a programmer and for an artist alike. If that is more than you want, clear the mark
  and give the record a skill — it will then reach reporting tasks only.
* **The source requirement** is worth narrowing down to what is actually verifiable for you: a
  list of «what counts as a primary source here» beats a general appeal.
* **The shape of the comparison table.** Write in your own mandatory columns (price, licence,
  lead time) — the rule becomes executable.
* **The install scope.** The pack fits the organisation's general rules as a whole: its records
  depend neither on the language nor on the subject area.
