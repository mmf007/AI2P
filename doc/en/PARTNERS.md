# Partnership, investment and contributing to the project (AI2P)

Welcome! The `mmf007/AI2P` project has been laying a solid legal and commercial foundation from the
very first day of development. We are open to cooperation both with independent developers and with
venture investors.

---

## Legal status and licensing

[![License: Apache 2.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Apache_2.0-blue.svg)](../../LICENSE)
[![CLA assistant](https://cla-assistant.io/readme/badge/mmf007/AI2P)](https://cla-assistant.io)

1. **The base license:** the source code of the core is distributed under the permissive
   **Apache 2.0** license. It gives the users the maximum freedom and is completely safe for
   commercial use.
2. **Protection of the intellectual property (CLA):** to keep the possibility of commercialization
   and to protect the rights of the investors, every third-party developer has to sign a
   *Contributor License Agreement* through the automatic **CLA Assistant** service when sending the
   first Pull Request. This guarantees that the project can be scaled up or relicensed in the future
   without obstacles.

---

## 💡 The business model (Open Core)

We plan to develop the project along a hybrid scheme:
* **Core (the open base):** always stays free and available to the community and to ordinary users
  under the Apache 2.0 license.
* **Enterprise (the corporate layer):** the additional modules (advanced security, audit logs,
  integrations, enterprise support) will be supplied under a closed commercial license on a paid
  basis.
